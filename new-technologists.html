A logo for the rebrand of Microsoft’s summer academy for young technologists, and the mark at the center of a year of program merch.

The New Technologists Summer Academy is a seven-week paid innovation academy for students pursuing degrees in technology — app development, product concepting, and hands-on training from senior staff inside the Microsoft workspace. In April of 2018 the program heads asked me for a mark to carry their rebrand from the Young Technologists Summer Academy. It went on t-shirts, balloons, mints, lanyards, and frisbees.
