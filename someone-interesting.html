A proximity app that points you toward one nearby stranger and tells you nothing else. No profile, no photo, no name, no map. Just a hand on your screen, pointing, and a distance that closes as you walk.

I designed and built the whole thing: the concept, the interface, the photography, and a front end that streams live position through a lightweight real-time backend.

This is an experiment in trust and social dynamics. It's a new way for strangers to engage: aware of each other in the same moment, one direction between them, and no way to learn more without moving.
