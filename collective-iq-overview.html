Collective IQ is a multimodal AI knowledge product built on M365 and Copilot. Large organizations hold an enormous amount of knowledge that is unused, most of it is locked inside meetings and the memories of the people who were there. Collective IQ turns that into something the wider organization can parse through and engage with.

As the design lead, I own the surface end to end, from the core interaction model through the web management portal, Teams app experience, and all production assets. The work centers on two problems: getting people to contribute knowledge they trust won't come back on them, and making a massive, fragmented body of information organized enough to surface as real insight.

This is a multi-faceted experience, spanning Meetings, Teams App, Copilot, Web, and more. The details of this project are under NDA. Please reach out to learn more or to see a full case study.
