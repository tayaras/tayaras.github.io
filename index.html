Tay Aras

Tay Aras is a designer, builder, and musician. His work explores connection: how we can bring people closer to each other, and to the things they care about.

At Microsoft, he builds virtual communication experiences and multimodal sharing products. He also runs Nowhere Interesting, an independent design studio and creative lab working with artists, musicians, and startups.

XR Experiences

Carnegie Mellon University

Physicalization of AR/XR Experiences

2022

Designed and built six physical prototyping tools for augmented reality, investigating how materials, object geometry, and sensor inputs affect the experience.

Six working tools combine fabricated objects, digital models, and electronics. Early tracking and alignment problems shifted the thesis from designing a complete experience to examining the mechanics that make it possible.

SOMEONEINTERESTING.ONLINE

Nowhere Interesting

someoneinteresting.online

2026

Designed and built an app that points toward another nearby participant, exploring whether curiosity alone could give strangers a reason to meet.

Created the interface, photography, motion, and real-time behavior. A photographed hand points toward another participant and changes gesture as the distance closes, offering direction without profiles or messaging.
