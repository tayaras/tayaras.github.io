Brand and identity for a Bellevue School Board campaign — a logo, a yard sign, and the guidelines that kept it consistent once it left my hands.

A local campaign has no brand team. Whatever gets made has to be reproducible by volunteers, on a deadline, across printers nobody controls. One wordmark, a fixed palette, and rules plain enough to follow without a designer in the room. It ran across two cycles, with only the verb changing from ELECT to RE-ELECT.
